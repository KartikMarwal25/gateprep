# Daily Planner: 28 Sep 2026 – 6 Feb 2027

One entry per day. Every subtopic GATE 2027 CS tests is covered by 10 January, followed by one revision week and three weeks of mocks. Tick each day when it's done; if you fall behind, use the next Sunday to catch up rather than shifting the whole plan.

Every topic below is from the official [GATE 2027 CS syllabus (IIT Madras)](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/CS_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf). Topics removed for 2027 (UDP, ARP/DHCP/ICMP, SMTP/FTP, framing, bridging, secondary storage) are left out. Each day names the playlist to watch; open it and play the videos whose titles match that day's topic.

**Every study day (about 6 hours):** 3.5 h lecture + short notes on the day's topic → 1.5 h PYQs on those exact subtopics from GATE Overflow → 30 min General Aptitude (this week's GA focus is under each week heading) → 10 min updating your error log.

**Every Sunday:** a timed test on the week's PYQs, then review the error log and finish anything left over. Include one "what does this code compute?" question, and check every option of each MSQ separately.
