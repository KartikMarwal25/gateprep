# Video Playlists

Every free YouTube playlist you need for GATE 2027 CS, grouped by subject and in the order of the 19-week plan. YouTube can't put playlists inside a playlist, so this page is the single hub. Open each link and tap Save to collect them all under Library on YouTube.

## 1. Engineering Mathematics

| Topic | Playlist | Watch for GATE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discrete Maths | [Discrete Mathematics — Gate Smashers](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLxCzCOWd7aiH2wwES9vPWsEL6ipTaUSl3) | Logic, sets, relations, functions, POSETs, lattices, groups, counting, recurrences |
| Graph Theory | [Graph Theory — Gate Smashers](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLxCzCOWd7aiG0M5FqjyoqB20Edk0tyzVt) | Connectivity, matching, colouring |
| Probability & Statistics | [Statistics and Probability — Gajendra Purohit](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLU6SqdYcYsfLRq3tu-g_hvkHDcorrtcBK) | Random variables, the five named distributions, mean/SD, Bayes |
| Linear Algebra | [MIT 18.06 — Gilbert Strang](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/) (course page with the full video list) | Elimination, LU, determinants, eigenvalues. Lectures 1–4, 18–22 are enough |
| Calculus | [Search: GATE CSE calculus](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=gate+cse+calculus+one+shot) | Limits, continuity, maxima–minima, MVT, integration. A single one-shot video covers it |

## 5. General Aptitude

Aptitude is learned by solving, not watching. Use the GA sections of past GATE papers, 10 questions a day, and look up a video only for a question type you can't crack.
